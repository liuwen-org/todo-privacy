todo / Tomato 隐私权政策

更新及生效日期：2026年10月19日 · 适用版本：1.8_1

开发者：liuwen-org

本政策适用于 todo 项目的 Android 番茄计时应用（当前应用内名称为 Tomato，包名：tech.liuwen.todo）。应用提供专注与休息计时、专注统计、提醒、桌面组件及备份恢复功能，无需注册账号，核心功能可离线使用。

一、应用处理的数据及用途

  • 专注统计：日期、每天各时段的累计专注时长和休息时长，用于显示历史记录、图表、目标进度及桌面组件。
  • 应用偏好：专注与休息时长、循环次数、每日目标、主题、语言、提醒声音及其引用、音量、振动、勿扰和显示设置，用于保存你的选择并运行计时功能。
  • 计时状态：当前模式、剩余时间及运行状态，用于计时、后台运行、通知和组件显示。
  • 备份文件：仅在你主动备份或恢复时，写入所选位置或读取所选文件，用于迁移本地统计和设置。

这些数据在设备本地处理。应用不要求提供姓名、邮箱、手机号或身份信息，不请求定位、通讯录、短信、通话记录、相机或麦克风权限。

二、联网、统计与第三方服务

当前版本不申请互联网访问权限，未接入广告、Google Analytics for Firebase、崩溃上报或其他第三方使用统计 SDK，不向开发者服务器上传专注记录、偏好、设备标识符或备份文件，不出售这些数据。应用内的“统计”是根据本地专注记录生成的功能。

应用没有开发者账号系统或应用自建的云同步。Android 系统备份、你选择的云盘文件服务、浏览器和邮件服务可能独立联网并按其各自规则处理数据，详见下文。

三、系统权限、通知及显示

  • 通知和实时进度通知：显示计时进度、控制按钮及计时结束提醒。前台服务用于在后台继续计时。
  • 振动：按你的设置提供触感及计时提醒。
  • 勿扰访问：仅在你授权并启用对应功能时，在专注过程中控制系统勿扰状态；不用于读取其他应用的通知内容。
  • 文件访问：通过 Android 系统文件选择器访问你选择的备份目录或恢复文件，不申请广泛存储访问权限。

你可以在应用或 Android 系统设置中调整相关功能及权限。拒绝或撤销授权可能影响对应提醒、勿扰或文件功能。通知及桌面组件可能显示在锁屏或主屏幕上，请通过系统设置管理可见范围。

四、手动备份与恢复

手动备份将本地应用数据库导出为 .db 文件，包含专注统计和保存在数据库中的偏好设置。应用不会对该文件额外进行密码加密。请将其保存在可信位置，避免公开分享。

文件保存在你通过系统选择器指定的位置。如果选择云盘或第三方文件服务，该服务可能上传、存储或同步文件，并按其自身隐私规则处理；这不表示开发者能够访问你的文件。恢复会用所选数据库替换当前本地数据库，原有记录及设置可能被覆盖，请先保存所需备份。

五、Android 系统备份和设备迁移

应用允许 Android 系统备份，当前备份规则未额外排除应用数据库及偏好等符合系统备份条件的数据。因此，系统可能将相关数据保存到系统备份服务或迁移至其他设备。是否备份、备份内容、保护方式及恢复行为取决于 Android 版本、设备厂商、备份服务和你的系统设置。

这些备份由操作系统或相应服务提供商处理，不发送给开发者。你可以通过系统及备份服务设置管理或删除备份。请勿将“本地处理”理解为数据在任何情况下都不会离开设备。

六、数据保存、删除及你的选择

本地统计和设置通常保留至你主动删除或清除应用数据。你可以在设置中使用“重置统计数据”永久删除已保存的统计；此操作不会清除全部偏好。通过 Android 系统清除应用存储数据或卸载应用，可以移除该安装的本地私有数据。

上述操作不会自动删除已导出的备份、系统备份、云盘文件及其他设备上的副本，需要在相应位置另行管理。系统恢复或重新导入旧备份可能重新带回之前的记录。应用没有账号，因此无需办理应用账号注销；开发者未持有你的本地记录，无法代为恢复或远程删除。

七、安全措施

本地私有数据受 Android 应用隔离和设备安全机制保护。当前应用没有额外的数据库密码加密或应用锁，手动备份也没有额外加密。请使用设备锁屏，妥善保管设备和备份，避免将备份放入公开目录。任何安全措施都不能保证绝对安全。

八、未成年人保护

应用不要求提供年龄或身份信息，也不通过广告或使用统计 SDK 收集未成年人的信息。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用，并谨慎管理设备、提醒显示及备份文件。

九、政策更新与本网页

应用功能或数据处理方式变化时，本政策将相应更新，并注明更新日期及适用版本。

本网页没有加入分析脚本、广告或跟踪 Cookie。访问本网页时，网页托管服务仍可能按其自身规则处理 IP 地址、浏览器信息及访问日志；这些网页访问数据与应用内本地专注统计不同。

十、联系我们

如有隐私问题、投诉或数据相关请求，请联系开发者：liuwen.yeah@gmail.com。你主动提供的邮箱、请求内容和附件将用于处理及回复请求；邮件服务商也会按其自身规则处理邮件。请勿发送完整备份、密码或不必要的敏感信息。

todo / Tomato Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: October 19, 2026 · Applies to version 1.8_1

Developer: liuwen-org

This policy covers the todo project's Android Pomodoro timer (currently named Tomato in the app; package: tech.liuwen.todo). It provides focus and break timers, focus statistics, reminders, home-screen widgets, and backup and restore. No account is required, and core features work offline.

1. Data handled and its purposes

  • Focus statistics: dates, accumulated focus durations for periods of each day, and break durations, used for history, charts, goal progress, and widgets.
  • Preferences: focus and break durations, cycle count, daily goal, theme, language, reminder sounds and their references, volume, vibration, Do Not Disturb, and display settings, used to remember choices and operate the timer.
  • Timer state: current mode, remaining time, and running status, used for timing, background operation, notifications, and widgets.
  • Backup files: written to a selected location or read from a selected file only when you initiate backup or restore, to transfer local statistics and settings.

These data are processed on your device. The app does not require your name, email address, phone number, or identity information, or request permissions for location, contacts, messages, call logs, camera, or microphone.

2. Network access, analytics, and third-party services

The current version does not request Internet permission and includes no advertising, Google Analytics for Firebase, crash-reporting, or other third-party usage analytics SDK. It does not upload focus records, preferences, device identifiers, or backup files to developer servers or sell these data. In-app statistics are generated from local focus records.

The app has no developer account system or app-operated cloud synchronization. Android system backup, cloud file services you select, browsers, and email services may independently access the network and process data under their own rules, as described below.

3. System permissions, notifications, and display

  • Notifications and live progress notifications show timer progress, controls, and completion reminders. A foreground service keeps the timer running in the background.
  • Vibration provides haptic feedback and timer reminders according to your settings.
  • Do Not Disturb access controls the system's Do Not Disturb state during focus sessions only when you grant access and enable the feature. It is not used to read other apps' notification contents.
  • File access uses Android's system file picker to access your selected backup directory or restore file, without requesting broad storage access.

You can adjust features and permissions in the app or Android settings. Denying or revoking access may affect the corresponding reminder, Do Not Disturb, or file feature. Notifications and widgets may appear on the lock screen or home screen; manage their visibility in system settings.

4. Manual backup and restore

Manual backup exports the local app database as a .db file containing focus statistics and preferences stored in the database. The app does not add password encryption to this file. Keep it in a trusted location and avoid sharing it publicly.

Files are saved to the location you select through the system picker. A selected cloud drive or third-party file service may upload, store, or synchronize the file under its own privacy rules; this does not give the developer access to it. Restore replaces the current local database with the selected database and may overwrite existing records and settings. Save any needed backup first.

5. Android system backup and device transfer

The app allows Android system backup. Current rules do not add exclusions for eligible app data such as the database and preferences. The system may therefore save these data to a system backup service or transfer them to another device. Whether backup occurs, its contents, protection, and restore behavior depend on Android, the device manufacturer, the backup service, and your settings.

The operating system or relevant provider handles these backups without sending them to the developer. Manage or delete backups through system and backup-service settings. Local processing does not mean data can never leave the device.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local statistics and settings generally remain until you delete them or clear app data. “Reset stats” in settings permanently deletes saved statistics but does not clear all preferences. Clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling removes that installation's private local data.

These actions do not automatically delete exported backups, system backups, cloud files, or copies on other devices; manage these separately. System restore or importing an old backup may bring back previous records. There is no app account to delete. The developer does not hold your local records and cannot recover or remotely delete them for you.

7. Security

Android app isolation and device security protect private local data. The current app provides no additional database password encryption or app lock; manual backups also have no additional encryption. Use a device screen lock, protect your device and backups, and avoid public backup locations. No security measure guarantees absolute protection.

8. Children

The app does not request age or identity information or collect children's information through advertising or usage analytics SDKs. Minors should use it with guidance from a parent or guardian and carefully manage their device, reminder visibility, and backup files.

9. Policy changes and this webpage

This policy will be updated when app features or data-handling practices change, with the updated date and applicable version shown above.

This webpage includes no analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies. The hosting service may still process IP addresses, browser information, and access logs under its own rules. Webpage access data are separate from the app's local focus statistics.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, complaints, or data requests, contact the developer at liuwen.yeah@gmail.com. The email address, request contents, and attachments you voluntarily provide will be used to handle and respond to your request. Email providers also process messages under their own rules. Do not send complete backups, passwords, or unnecessary sensitive information.
